What we do

Mizu Skin is a small skincare brand built on one idea: a good routine should be short, gentle and easy to follow. We make cleansers, serums, moisturisers and sunscreens with a short list of active ingredients, no heavy fragrance and clear labels.

Every product is tested for comfort on sensitive skin and priced so you can build a full routine without overspending.

About us

We started Mizu Skin as a college project with a simple question: why is it so hard to find skincare that is both effective and easy to understand?

  • Short ingredient lists, with the active percentage on the front of the pack
  • Fragrance-free options in every category
  • Cruelty-free and dermatologically reviewed formulas
  • Recyclable packaging wherever possible
