MIZU SKIN - student e-commerce website (6 pages)
=================================================
Files: index.html, cleansers.html, serums.html, moisturisers.html, sunscreen.html,
contact.html, style.css, script.js

BEFORE YOU PUBLISH - EDIT THESE
1. Brand name: do a find-and-replace of "Mizu Skin" (and "mizuskin") with your own name in all .html files.
2. Contact details: contact.html and the footer of every page (address, email, phone).
   Also change the email in script.js (search "hello@mizuskin.example").
3. Social links: contact.html (Instagram/Facebook/X/YouTube) - paste your real page URLs.
4. Google Analytics: open script.js, replace G-XXXXXXXXXX at the top with your GA4 Measurement ID.

PUBLISH FREE ON GITHUB PAGES
1. Sign in at github.com, click New repository, name it "website", set it Public.
2. Click "uploading an existing file", drag in ALL files from this folder, then Commit.
3. Go to Settings > Pages > Source: "Deploy from a branch" > Branch: main / (root) > Save.
4. After about a minute your link is: https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/website/
